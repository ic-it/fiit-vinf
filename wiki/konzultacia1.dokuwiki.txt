====== First consultation - sources and questions ======

[[user:icit:start|Project page]]

The initial goal was to search fish species using names, descriptions and biological facts. The first submission implements full-text search; numerical attribute filters are not implemented.

**Initial source:** [[https://www.fishbase.se/search.php|FishBase]], its linked scientific-name catalogs and English species summaries. The target fields are scientific/common names, taxonomy, environment, distribution, reported maximum length and available IUCN status.

**Planned Wikipedia source:** [[https://dumps.wikimedia.org/enwiki/|English Wikipedia dump]]. Example articles include [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_bream|Common bream]], [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterlet|Sterlet]] and [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphiprion_ocellaris|Ocellaris clownfish]]. A later stage will identify candidate matches by scientific name and verify redirects, identifiers and taxonomic agreement. Descriptions and match evidence will remain separate from source records.

===== Initial questions =====

These are motivating questions and candidate source pages, not verified results from the current development sample. Current observed text queries and results are in the [[user:icit:odovzdanie1|first-submission report]]. The length-threshold example below is an initial idea, not an implemented query operator.

^ Question ^ Candidate source / expected kind of answer ^
| Which freshwater fish occur in Europe? | [[https://fishbase.se/summary/SpeciesSummary.php?id=268&lang=English|Abramis brama]]; environment and distribution. |
| Which tropical reef fish have reported maximum length below 15 cm? | [[https://fishbase.se/summary/SpeciesSummary.php?id=6509&lang=English|Amphiprion ocellaris]]; habitat and length. |
| Which critically endangered species occur in the Mekong? | [[https://fishbase.se/summary/SpeciesSummary.php?id=16239&lang=English|Aaptosyax grypus]]; distribution and IUCN status. |
| What maximum length is reported for sterlet? | [[https://fishbase.se/summary/SpeciesSummary.php?id=4604&lang=English|Acipenser ruthenus]]; length and measurement type. |
| Which temperate freshwater fish occur in China? | [[https://fishbase.se/summary/SpeciesSummary.php?id=52749&lang=English|Siniperca obscura]]; environment, climate and distribution. |

The current search accepts words, quoted phrases and Boolean expressions. It does not interpret arbitrary natural-language questions. Additional fish websites can later contribute their own extraction adapters and source-qualified records.
